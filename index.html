Bare landto harvest

Every Farmer Fay project follows the same four steps, so you always know what comes next and what it costs.

  1. Consult

    We visit your site, test the soil and water, and listen to what you want from the farm.

  2. Plan

    Crop selection, layout, budget and a planting calendar matched to the rains or your irrigation.

  3. Build

    Land preparation, greenhouse or open-field setup, and irrigation installed and tested.

  4. Grow

    Planting, crop care and management, with support from transplant to harvest and beyond.

The woman behind the brand

FavourDavid

Founder & CEO, Farmer Fay

Favour David started Farmer Fay with a simple belief: a farm deserves the same care, order and style as any modern business.

She works hands-on with first-time farmers, professionals and investors across Nigeria and abroad, and travels to set up farms wherever her clients are. Farmer Fay is her proof that you can love pink and still get your hands deep in the soil.

“Every farm deserves a plan, and every farmer deserves to be proud of what they grow.”Favour David
